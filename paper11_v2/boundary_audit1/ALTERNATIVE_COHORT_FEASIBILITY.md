## Candidate 1: Natural Object Recognition

The2025descriptor reports16patients at WVU,WUSTL andUtah, amygdala/hippocampal
single-neuron recordings,1second one-back image presentations, and public OSF
spike/event data plus raw continuous recordings. These sites differ from the
primary Zurich cohort and DANDI000004sites; that is a promising independence
boundary, not verified private-patient nonoverlap. Different cognitive tasks
permit convergence only, not direct replication.
[Descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-04265-1),
[OSF accession](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/VH5KQ).

Prospective feasibility steps before neural access:pin OSF revision/file list
and licenses; verify patient/session identifiers and prior exposure; request
independent raw recording headers/gap logs plus event-to-recording clock map;
establish hippocampus-only unit/session labels and simultaneous populations.
Then independently register calibration/final boundaries and train-only QC.

spikes/unit must actually pass frozen admission, not be inferred from16published
patients or total MTL unit counts. Do not mix ImageNet/COCO patient sessions as
independent patients. Keep original DANDI60/40unchanged and separately locked.

## Candidate 2: Multielement Episodic Memory

The2023study reports30participants across Birmingham/Erlangen and provides
data/code at Figshare. Separate institutions are promising for independence;
anatomy, simultaneous-session populations, task epochs and complete recording
support still require audit. Associative encoding/recall differs from both
primary working memory and object recognition; any validation is convergent.
[Study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-023-01706-6),
[Figshare accession](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.14905317).

Do not assume that all published participants have five simultaneous hippocampal
units, that subject labels are unique across experiments, or that public files
include unselected continuous spikes/observation masks. A new metadata-only
inspection must check file versions, patient overlap, population identity,
per-trial training/calibration feasibility and native acquisition clocks before
prospective train-only unit QC. Patient, never session/trial/unit, is independent.
