Old successful synthetic fits have no prospective roster because they precede this completeness repair. Their saved fixture counts may reconstruct a roster only under the explicit RETROSPECTIVE_SAVED_SYNTHETIC label. This is prohibited for real C. The dormant future broker is fail-closed without separately authorized real evidence and a registered producer.
