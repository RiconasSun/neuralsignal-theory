# Scope and limitations of the R3 Development study

This explanatory handoff does not amend the normative M1 documents, prospective
S07/N4 contract, Phase A/B freezes, numerical policy or scientific stop rules.
It supplies no outcome-dependent decision or new hypothesis.

The unchanged source cohort contains 28 original Development mice, 27 admitted
mice (5 BO, 22 FC), and 27 reserved Final BO mice. The previously excluded mouse
is not replaced. These are source cohorts, not randomized protocol assignments;
BO/FC descriptive differences cannot establish a causal protocol effect.

The independent statistical unit is the mouse. Predictions concern the original
selected simultaneous population, presentations, masks and score window. A
successful fit, higher predictive score or latent-state representation does not
establish a causal mechanism, physical neural state, CWP/NAP validation,
independent-laboratory replication or a Final result. Development evidence is
exploratory. No Final C/D access is authorized here.

The metadata repair retains the distinction between stimulus invalidity and
probe/neural support. It neither replaces stimulus validity with main_valid nor
changes the old archives. Twenty-two saved complete native interval inventories
certify absence of invalid_times; five contain the labeled table. The frozen
native-header producer verified numeric interval types, but did not serialize
their original HDF5 bit widths. Those widths are not newly asserted. The original
M1 numeric conversion and the actual saved float64 metadata are retained; exact
applicable intervals, explicit stimulus fields and original main masks were
independently checked. Curated source support is not proof of complete AP-packet
coverage or absence of every unrecorded artifact.

During B extraction the frozen trusted reader temporarily decoded each selected
unit's complete source timestamp row for hash/finiteness integrity checks,
including non-B periods, and exported only B. Thus zero E exports/evaluations
does not mean that E-period raw timestamps were never decoded internally. E was
not claimed, exported, binned or scored. This limitation concerns Development
recordings; the separate reserved Final C/D sources were not accessed.

Running precision and source identity remain governed by the inherited native
recovery evidence. No source values, scientific masks or precision standards are
redefined in this mission. Missing verified pupil-exposure timing cannot be
treated as absence of arousal. Panel availability is frozen before any scaler or
model fit; unavailable panels remain visible with their original reasons.

The B1-only B selection uses the original three-point ridge grid and balanced
BO/FC weighting. It is not an E-based model-selection procedure. All registered
D/S seeds are mandatory; a favorable seed cannot replace a failed seed. Original
PG, independent AD, fixed FD and budget gates remain separate requirements.
Optimizer success alone is insufficient. A required scientific failure consumes
this stage; subsequent work is saved-evidence closeout, not a repaired trial.

Different score-bin widths are not interchangeable nats-per-bin estimands.
Actual exposure and unit-bin denominators, plus the registered per-second
conversion for bin-width sensitivity, must accompany any completed results.
Reference operators N1/N3 remain approximate descriptive controls, not exact
conditional-null evidence. No failed or unavailable control may be relabeled
scientific PASS. Final-specific inference and positive-claim gates remain
unexecuted at the protected Final boundary.

R1's original STOP and two warnings, R2's consumed failed scientific stage and
one bug/one warning, and the first R3 pre-scientific ID-encoding failure all remain
historical evidence. Accepted metadata and production reviews do not
retrospectively clear those failures or prove real convergence. Only the final
execution receipts establish which R3 tasks and evaluations actually occurred.
