# Closure Scope

Only the new Boundary Audit namespace, mission specification, mission evidence,
and append-only ops/daily-operations.md update are staged. Original scientific
protocols, gates, splits and results remain unchanged.

The whole staged whitespace check detects whitespace already present in the
verbatim upstream source receipts under boundary_audit1/results/sources. Those
bytes are immutable provenance, not locally authored implementation. They are
not reformatted, and their registered hashes remain the integrity oracle.
The authored-file whitespace check excludes only that source-receipt directory.
This exclusion changes no test, scientific criterion or audit result.

Publication is limited to INCONCLUSIVE audit and STOP documentation. It is not
mission PASS, scientific PASS, R2 continuation, or main-branch promotion.
