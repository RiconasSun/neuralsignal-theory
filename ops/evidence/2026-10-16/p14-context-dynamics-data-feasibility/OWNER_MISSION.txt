## SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE

Begin Paper 14 of Computational Cognitive Primitive Neurodynamics (CCPN).

Working title:

"Context-Dependent Predictive Dynamics in Mouse Prefrontal Cortex"

Primary research question:

Does the predictive structure of local prefrontal neural population activity vary systematically across experimentally defined task or behavioral contexts, beyond what can be explained by firing rates, spike history, task events and measured nuisance variables?

P14 investigates **Context × Neural Dynamics**.

It does not assume that:
- Discrete state models must win.
- P13's descriptive results establish a biological mechanism.
- Context-dependent prediction implies causal cognitive computation.
- CWP/NAP primitives are already validated.

Positive, negative and inconclusive outcomes are equally admissible.

## M0.1 — DEFINE CANDIDATE CONTEXT STRUCTURES

Evaluate the feasibility of these context types, in priority order:

A. Within-animal task-context or prior-probability blocks.

B. Within-animal stimulus-history or task-phase conditions.

C. Recorded behavioral-state conditions, only when reliable state measurements and temporal alignment are available.

Prefer comparisons where the same mouse contributes neural recordings under both contexts.

The primary target must involve at least two clearly defined, independently identifiable contexts.

Do not substitute a between-animal group difference for a within-animal context effect without an explicit alternative design and confounding assessment.

Distinguish:
- Context labels known before prediction.
- Context inferred retrospectively from behavior.
- Context inferred from neural activity itself.

Do not use future behavioral or neural outcomes as supposedly causal predictors.

## M0.4 — CONTEXT IDENTIFIABILITY

For each candidate, determine whether context variation is scientifically distinguishable from:

- Passage of time and session drift
- Stimulus identity/intensity
- Choice and motor execution
- Reward or punishment history
- Trial-order effects
- Recording-quality changes
- Behavioral state or arousal
- Animal/session/batch differences

Inspect whether both contexts have adequate overlap in the relevant nuisance covariates.

A context label perfectly confounded with recording session, genotype or chronological order cannot automatically support a context-dependent neural-dynamics interpretation.

Report what is measured, controllable, partially confounded or unavailable.

Do not claim causality from observational differences.

## M0.5 — FUTURE MODEL FEASIBILITY

Evaluate whether each candidate could support a rigorous comparison among:

B0: Stationary firing-rate model.

B1: Context-aware task/time/nuisance baseline.

B2: Causal own-spike-history baseline.

B3: Causal population-history baseline.

B4: Strong combined context/history baseline.

D: Tractable discrete-state predictive representation.

C: Tractable continuous-history predictive representation.

Context-dependent versus context-invariant variants must be comparable under prospectively fixed complexity accounting.

The key future estimand should examine whether the incremental predictive gain from a dynamical representation differs across contexts, after strong context-aware baselines.

For example:

Δ_context =
[LL(Dynamics) − LL(Baseline)]_ContextA
−
[LL(Dynamics) − LL(Baseline)]_ContextB

This is a proposed estimand, not an observed effect.

Determine whether the available dataset supports:
- Within-mouse paired comparisons
- Training/calibration separated from scoring
- Independent held-out animals
- Causal state prediction
- Appropriate null controls and nuisance comparisons

Do not fit any model in M0.

No direct inheritance of numerically unstable P13 B* autoregressive simulation as a mandatory control.

Future nulls must have numerically validated, scientifically defensible definitions before they become final-evaluation prerequisites.
