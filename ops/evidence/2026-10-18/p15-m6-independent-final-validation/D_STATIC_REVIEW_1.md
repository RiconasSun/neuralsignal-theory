## P1-1 — Saved archive and attempted-fit completeness are not enforced

saved_verify.run iterates whatever archive hashes and fit files happen to be present. It does not require an exact non-work package file set, nonempty mandatory hash keys, the exact planned main/sensitivity endpoint identities, nor a stop/partial-ledger reconciliation that identifies permitted unattempted rows. An empty/omitted archive map passes its hash loops; a planned certificate missing from both disk and an incomplete manifest is never visited. A completed D result can therefore be reported as successfully reproduced with fewer endpoint certificates than its frozen C plans require. This is a saved-evidence completeness defect, even if the future archive writer intends to list every file.

Required repair: exact archive membership with documented exclusions/required artifacts, exact complete-stage plan/attempt sets and state/configuration identity, and explicit legitimate stop/partial-attempt accounting when no completed D assessment exists. Empty maps, missing planned endpoints, extra/substituted paths and inconsistent completion markers must fail. Preserve partial failures; do not invent endpoints or execute new fits to fill gaps.

## P1-2 — Saved score/reference arithmetic and schedules are trusted rather than verified

For completed D, the verifier rebuilds only summaries from saved mouse/case/control panels. It does not independently validate source-bound exposure denominators, original-control observed vectors from original_model_scores, each replicate effect from saved model scores, 999 journal records/replicate identities/seeds/mappings, per-suite coverage or summary-journal agreement. control_summary trusts observed/reference_effects directly. analyze_final checks equal positive pair denominators but not their expected trial/bin/unit exposure. Thus mutually self-consistent cached summaries can survive a wrong numerator, denominator or reference schedule. Hash consistency alone cannot establish these numerical definitions.

- Six complete-labelled control panels with original saved B4/B1 scores implying gain0, cached observed gain9, zero completed replicates and empty seed/mapping lists are accepted by control_summary. They yield observed mean9 and a passing disruption gate from the cached vectors.
- Six main panels with60 trial ordinals but unit_bins=1 are accepted by analyze_final; they yield a self-consistent gain of1 despite an impossible registered eight-unit/ten-score-bin exposure.

Required repair: saved-only checks deriving original/replicate effects from finite saved LL/Q, exact main/case exposure/mask identities, exact native per-mouse/case paths/content and C-state hashes, registered per-suite cohort/exposure, all999 seed/donor/offset hashes and append-only journal events. Enforce N3's copied B4-LL statistic and separate B4-B1 diagnostic, unavailable suites and fixed p=1. Recompute existing saved arithmetic/random mappings only; no predictor, D array or likelihood replay is authorized by this audit requirement. The analogous independently reviewed R1 saved_audit pattern may be reused with the Final scope and conditional cohorts preserved.

These probes exercise existing pure statistical functions; the still-incomplete actual final saved reproduction was not run. The empty-manifest acceptance path is established statically, not falsely represented as an executed full real-data reproduction.
