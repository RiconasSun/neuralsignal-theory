# R2 production specification and execution ordering

Exact frozen M1 equations and additive S07/N4 definitions control the code. No
scientific value, source, unit, grid, seed or acceptance threshold is amended.
M1 objectives/analytic derivatives and separately coded dual-number forward AD are
version-verified imports. Production scoring for D/S is a separate forward-only
function: evaluation never calculates a backward smoother or evaluation gradient.

The common A feature union supplies identical named scaler/active-map entries to
all nested families. Unit-specific inactive nuisance coefficients are dropped by
packing; D/S expand them as literal zeros only to call the unchanged M1 objective.
Only free coordinates enter the optimizer and fixed FD budget. Unscaled PSTH
columns never drop. D/S initialization draws delta in ascending numeric unit-ID
order, then the three logits, at every registered seed. Sensitivity subset
selection and canonical parameter ordering are recorded separately.

All A raw features and prefit metadata/A-only panel subsets are constructed only
after actual M3 acceptance. S07 rates use the common main A scored targets, never
QC early spikes. Exact panel availability and task allocation are frozen before
fitting any scaler or model. Main and panel scalers are then fitted on their own A
rows. S05 is unavailable on sources without a frozen verified pupil-exposure-end
certificate; unknown timing is not absence of pupil/arousal and cannot become a
numerical-failure excuse. Its positive mathematical feature branch is tested on
synthetic verified timing. S10/S11 use original main units only. No unit replacement.

Every main B1 candidate at all three ridge values is certified before B source
access. B constructs B1 nuisances/targets only; no B4/C/D/S gain is evaluated.
Both BO/FC means and the tie rule are the unchanged M1 policy. Winning B1 states
are reused. All remaining main and registered available panel A fits and all
three D/S seeds are mandatory before a complete immutable M4 state seal. The
seal includes preprocessing, coefficients, all seeds, certificates, call and
accepted-vector journals, the exact task plan and B score curves. E is claimed
once before its first neural access, and only after verifying that complete seal.

The original source reader is reused through exact schema/value allowlists.
Only original Development identities are permitted. A uses old protected A
captures without reacquisition or new physiology. B/E full-row integrity decoding
is transient and checked against the original frozen row hash, then only the
authorized role is exported. Full E flash tails are retained only where the
prefit N3 support plan permits that registered operator. Verified local complete
R3/R4 caches are used first; any new R2 range has its own durable reservation,
exact ETag/206/range checks and no retry or alternative source.

The SciPy child has no features or counts. An external parent broker reserves and
denies each FG before evaluation6001, records accepted iterates and denies work
after iteration1500. A native non-breakaway Windows Job supervises the scientific
worker and every child for one48h boot/UTC/monotonic interval. There is no stage
reset. Separate fixed derivative counters allow exactly24P FD objective calls,
three independent AD calls and three analytic checkpoints; the authoritative PG
uses the same saved endpoint checkpoint gradient. Central FD may leave parameter
bounds only in the derivative oracle. All fixed scales are reported; h1e-5 alone
is the frozen FD acceptance scale. No favorable post-result scale is selected.

One E batch includes all eight main scores and six contrasts. Refit panels use
only their five registered model families; the two unregistered panel-specific
B2/B3 refits are not invented or charged. Main/fixed panels retain all required
contrasts. N1 copies the donor's complete joint raw history-feature block at the
same relative time index, including C kernels, with recipient targets/nuisances
unchanged; strata are visited by first original ordinal and their members in
ascending original ordinal. This implements aligned trajectories without adding
binary64 boundary error through unnecessary timestamp subtraction/addition. N2
iterates original presentation then numeric Unit ID and preserves main20ms
targets/history/B-D-S predictions; C may change. N3 independently shifts each unit
in the full native flash, retaining counts. N4 follows exactly the11 registered
raw-column maps before the frozen scaler. No operator fits or selects a model.

Reference-tail/Holm2 summaries are descriptive approximations, never exact
conditional-null p-values. Whole-mouse BO/FC10000 resamples use16043/16044; pooled
exploratory intervals retain16042. Final-specific coverage/positive-claim gates
remain NOT_EXECUTED_FINAL_BOUNDARY. No VALIDATED, causal, physical-state, CWP/NAP,
or independent-laboratory claim follows from engineering success or a positive gain.
