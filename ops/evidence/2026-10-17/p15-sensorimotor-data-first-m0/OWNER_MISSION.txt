## M0.6 — SENSORIMOTOR SCIENTIFIC QUESTION SELECTION

Depending on actual data availability, evaluate which primary question is best supported.

Candidate A:

Does local motor/somatosensory neural history improve prediction beyond strong movement, task and firing-rate baselines?

Candidate B:

Do compact discrete-state or continuous-history representations provide additional predictive structure around motor preparation and execution?

Candidate C:

Is there a reproducible transition in local predictive structure across documented sensory and motor task epochs?

These are alternative designs.

Choose ONE primary question on scientific relevance, recording support and feasible nuisance control.

Do not select the question based on favorable neural model results.

Do not assume an HMM wins or that physical neural primitives exist.

If windows are retrospectively aligned to movement onset, explicitly characterize the estimand as offline event-conditioned prediction, not real-time movement forecasting.

All eventual predictors must use only information available before their prediction bin.
