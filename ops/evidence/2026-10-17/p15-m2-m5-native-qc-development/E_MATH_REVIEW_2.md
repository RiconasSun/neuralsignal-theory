## S07 achieved A rate fields

The new fields are descriptive outputs after the existing A thinning and epoch binning: each unit's sum of thinned counts over all A warm-plus-score bins divided by `len(ids)*(warmup+.5)` seconds, plus that exposure in seconds. For S07, warmup=.5 and 20 bins of .05 seconds make this exactly the total retained A epoch exposure. An independent synthetic arithmetic check confirmed equivalence to `n_epochs*n_bins*.05`. The numerator is a per-unit count, so the denominator correctly omits the number of units. This is an achieved finite-sample epoch rate, not a promise of exactly 2 Hz or a whole-role rate.

The added reporting fields do not alter thinning probabilities, random stream, event order, units, QC/ranking, A scales, masks, fit configurations or source targets.
